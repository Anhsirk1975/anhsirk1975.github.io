XspineFlask

XspineFlask is a private, internal billing and reporting system built for Xspine Tech. This page exists to satisfy Google's OAuth application requirements, enabling XspineFlask to securely save its own automatically generated reports to a private Google Drive folder controlled by the account owner. XspineFlask is not a public product and is not available for use by external users.

Privacy Policy

This application requests access to Google Drive using the restrictive "drive.file" scope, which limits access exclusively to files and folders that this application itself creates. XspineFlask does not access, read, or modify any other files in the user's Google Drive account. Data accessed through this integration is used solely to store automatically generated business reports (billing, GST, and financial reports) for the account owner's own private use. No data is shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. This application is for the private, internal use of a single account holder and is not distributed publicly.
